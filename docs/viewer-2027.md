# RoboMaster 2027 viewer

[2026 mechanisms and joint controls](viewer.md) · [2027 downloads](releases/2026-10-15.md) · [简体中文](zh/viewer-2027.md)

Inspect the RMUC 2027 arena, its individual assemblies, the RMUL 3v3 and 1v1
arenas, and the energy unit. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Select a model
above the view, then use **Fit view** to frame it.

<ModelViewer catalog-path="/models/2027/catalog.json" initial-model="rmuc2027-full-map" />

These 21 models come from the official 2027 CAD releases. They retain the
source rest pose and source colors. Mechanical joint bindings have not been
established, so the 2027 catalog has no joint sliders. The
[2026 viewer](viewer.md) retains its movable mechanisms and controls.

The complete arena already contains its source placements. Individual
assemblies use their own local frames. See the [2027 build guide](rm2027.md)
for coordinates, validation scope, and archive reproduction.
